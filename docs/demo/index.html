Challenge: Space Mission Design Game

Real missions juggle science goals, mass, power, budget, communications and orbits. For most students those trade-offs stay abstract. We built a game where you plan, build and fly a whole mission and see how every choice shapes the result.

The idea

A fading signal from an icy moon

A radio pulse is coming from Ceruvia, 1.4 billion km away. To earn the trip, your team flies five missions, each teaching a new idea.

🌍 Earth orbitmass, power, budget
🌕 The Moonfar side, landing
🪨 Asteroidsample return
🔴 Marsdust storms, RTGs
🧊 Ceruviaeverything at once
  • No single right answer: a heavy design works with a bigger rocket, a cheap one with a slower route.
  • Beginner mode is very hard to fail; Flight Director is a real challenge.
  • Runs in any browser, on phones too. No install, no account.

Fly it, then do the science

Six flight minigames, real consequences

  • Launch, debris field, orbit insertion, landing, touch-and-go and reentry.
  • Your design changes the flight: heavy craft steer slowly, shields soak up hits.
  • Choose where to scan, survive a power emergency, then read a report with real missions like yours.
  • Co-op for 2–4 players with crew roles and a GO / NO GO vote.

Impact and what's next

Every student can be a mission designer

  • Learn by doing: trade-offs become choices you feel, not formulas you memorise.
  • For everyone: plain language, a coach, autopilots, keyboard or touch.
  • Open: Apache-2.0, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, nothing to install.
  • Next: a classroom mode, more targets (Europa, Titan) and real ephemeris data for trajectories.
